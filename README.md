# THE MALOUZ PROJECT — ATHENS ATELIER

An avant-garde, brutalist digital showroom and archive for **The Malouz Project** ([@mmalouz](https://www.instagram.com/mmalouz?stkn=MWV0em5hdGxzZHNpbA==)), an independent artist and maker based in Athens, Greece.

---

## 👁️ CONCEPT & ARTISTIC PHILOSOPHY

The Malouz Project rejects sterile, mass-manufactured lifestyles. Rooted in the raw concrete and subcultural pulse of Athens, the atelier produces tactile artifacts that celebrate flesh, pressure, and extraterrestrial eroticism.

### The 5 Sexual Alien Figures

Every garment, vessel, and drawing in the atelier is structured around five anatomical alien archetypes:

1. **RADIENTS (The Spinal Pulsar):** Concentric vertebral ribs radiating bioluminescent solar-sexual heat along the spine.
2. **ORIENTS (The Cardinal Alignment):** Intersecting limbs locked at 90-degree angular friction, merging geometric architecture with wet human intimacy.
3. **NAVIENS (The Abyssal Hollow):** Undulating pelvic cavities and fluid sensual gill apertures capturing subterranean desire.
4. **CERTIENS (The Armored Carapace):** Brutalist interlocking skeletal plates protecting sensitive neural flesh.
5. **LVIENS (The Zero-G Suspension):** Weightless sinuous helical tendrils capturing ethereal climax unmoored from terrestrial gravity.

## 📦 ATELIER COLLECTIONS

- **Alien T-Shirt Drops:** Heavyweight 300–340gsm raw Greek combed cotton with high-density silver plastisol, 3D silicone carapace embossing, and water-based sumi discharge inks.
- **Sculptural Ceramics:** High-grogg volcanic black stoneware, cone 10 reduction, and pit-fired offering dishes with hand-carved incisions.
- **Handmade Architectural Bags:** 24oz waxed paraffin canvas, full-grain bridle leather, hand-hammered copper rivets, and anodized aluminum cobra buckles.
- **Drawings & Archival Folios:** Sumi ink, carbon wash, and technical drafting pens on 400gsm Arches deckled cotton sheets.

## 🛠️ TECH STACK

- **Framework:** React 18 + Vite 6
- **Language:** TypeScript 5.7
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 3.4 (Custom dark brutalist palette & architectural grid)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Audio Atmosphere:** Web Audio API Procedural Synthesizer (55Hz sub drone + kiln resonance + analog tape texture)
- **Deployment:** Vercel Edge CDN
